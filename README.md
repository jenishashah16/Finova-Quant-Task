# FINOVA Quant & Trading Recruitment Task

## Strategy
20/50 EMA Crossover Strategy

## Instrument
HDFC Bank (HDFCBANK)

## Methodology
- Timeframe: 1D
- Testing period: Last 12 months
- Entry: 20-day EMA crosses above 50-day EMA
- Exit: 20-day EMA crosses below 50-day EMA
- Entry/exit price: Closing price of the crossover candle

## Results

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|

## Verdict

**HDFCBANK | 3 Trades | 33.33% Win Rate | I would not trade this strategy with my own money yet.**

The 20/50 EMA crossover strategy gave mixed results for HDFC Bank. Out of the three trades, only one was profitable, with a return of 9.12%, while the other two resulted in losses of 1.69% and 6.72%. So although the strategy was able to capture a good upward move, it did not give consistent results in this period. Also, since there were only three trades, the sample size is too small to confidently say that the strategy works or does not work.

One major weakness of this strategy is that EMA crossovers are delayed signals. By the time the crossover happens, some of the price movement may have already taken place. The strategy would probably work better when the stock is in a clear and sustained trend, but could perform poorly when the market is moving sideways or changing direction frequently. Because of this, I would not use the 20/50 EMA crossover alone with real money and would test it on a larger set of data before making a decision.
